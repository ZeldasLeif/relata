relata

A quiet canvas for the relationships in your world.

Write
Click a title or text area and type. Select text for bold, italic, underline (or Ctrl/⌘ B · I · U).
Move
Press and drag a node.
Relate
Drag from a node's edge. Drop on another node to connect them, or on empty space to create a new connected node.
Create
Double-click empty space, or right-click for a typed node.
Focus
Click a type (on a node or up top) to show only that type. Click empty space to show everything.
Images
Drop or paste an image onto a node, or right-click › Add image. It appears as its own image node, attached to and named after that node.
Story
Open the story panel (C), add chapters, drag nodes onto them and order them. P plays the story in order with ← →.
Connections
Double-click a line to label it. Click it and press Delete to remove.
Navigate
Scroll to zoom, drag empty space (or hold Space) to pan. F fits all, 0 centers the first node.
Undo
Ctrl/⌘ Z · redo Ctrl/⌘ Shift Z. Right-click a node to change its type or delete it.
Mentions
Write another node's exact title in a text and it becomes an underlined link. Click it to jump there (Ctrl/⌘-click while writing).
Lines
Right-click a connection to change its color, thickness or dash.
Projects
Edit the project title at the top right; the ⌄ next to it switches or creates projects.
Saving
Every change is saved automatically — into your data folder as .json (Chrome/Edge), otherwise inside this browser. Daily snapshots go to data/backups/.
