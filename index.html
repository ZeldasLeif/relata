relata

A quiet canvas for the relationships in your world.

Write
Click a title or text area and type. Select text for bold, italic, underline (or Ctrl/⌘ B · I · U).
Move
Press and drag a node.
Relate
Drag from a node's edge. Drop on another node to connect them, or on empty space to create a new connected node.
Create
Double-click empty space, or right-click for a typed node.
Focus
Click a type (on a node or up top) to show only that type. Click empty space to show everything.
Images
Drop or paste an image onto a node, or right-click › Add image.
Connections
Double-click a line to label it. Click it and press Delete to remove.
Navigate
Scroll to zoom, drag empty space (or hold Space) to pan. F fits all, 0 centers the first node.
Undo
Ctrl/⌘ Z · redo Ctrl/⌘ Shift Z. Right-click a node to change its type or delete it.
Saving
Everything is saved automatically in this browser. Use Export for backups or to move to another browser.
